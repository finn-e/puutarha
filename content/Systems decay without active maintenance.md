# Systems decay without active maintenance

The second law of thermodynamics applies to software, habits, and physical infrastructure: without continuous energy input, order degrades into chaos. This absolute truth is why [[Evergreen notes require maintenance]].[^1]

[^1]: Brooks, F. P. (1975). The Mythical Man-Month.
