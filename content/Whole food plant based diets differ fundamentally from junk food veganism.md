# Whole food plant based diets differ fundamentally from junk food veganism

An ethical vegan who subsists entirely on processed faux-meats and refined oils will still suffer from poor metabolic health. Achieving longevity requires committing to intact grains, tubers, and legumes.[^1]

[^1]: Greger, M. (2015). How Not to Die. Flatiron Books.
