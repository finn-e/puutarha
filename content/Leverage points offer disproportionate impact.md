# Leverage points offer disproportionate impact

Finding the exact right node in a complex network allows a very small intervention to radically shift the behavior of the entire structure. In city planning, [[Infrastructure defines urban behavior]] by acting as a massive systemic lever.

[^1]: Meadows, D. H. (1999). Leverage Points: Places to Intervene in a System.
