# Saturated fats from animal products are a primary driver of atherosclerosis

The accumulation of plaque within arterial walls is heavily accelerated by the consumption of animal-derived lipoproteins, physically narrowing the vessels and restricting blood flow. This causes catastrophic systemic bottlenecks.[^1]

[^1]: Campbell, T. C. (2006). The China Study.
