# Mechanics of Extruder Gear Clicking

Rhythmic clicking in an extruder assembly usually signifies 'skipping steps.' This happens when the magnetic force between the motor's rotor and stator is overcome by physical torque. Common causes include debris in the gear teeth (filament disks), a seized bearing, or excessive backpressure from a clogged nozzle. If clicking occurs without filament loaded, the issue is strictly internal to the drive housing.
