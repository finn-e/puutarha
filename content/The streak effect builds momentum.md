# The streak effect builds momentum

Maintaining an unbroken chain of daily actions becomes a powerful psychological motivator in itself, shifting the focus from the outcome to the process. Games understand this deeply, as [[Gamification loops hijack the brains completionist tendencies]].[^1]

[^1]: Seinfeld, J. (Commonly attributed 'Don't break the chain').
