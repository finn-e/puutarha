# Polymer thermal expansion dictates 3D printed keyboard tolerances

When printing interlocking switch plates, one must account for the natural shrinkage of cooling plastic. A hole modeled at exactly 14mm will print too small as the material contracts. Recognizing this physical reality ensures tight fits.[^1]

[^1]: Prusa Research. Material Thermal Properties.
