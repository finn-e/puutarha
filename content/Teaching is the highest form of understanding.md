# Teaching is the highest form of understanding

To effectively instruct someone else, you must successfully simplify, structure, and anticipate questions regarding the material. This acts as the ultimate test of mastery. [[Open-source communities thrive on asynchronous collaboration]] through mutual teaching.

[^1]: Seneca. (approx. 65 AD). 'Docendo discimus' (By teaching, we learn).
