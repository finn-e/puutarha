# High-quality infrastructure is invisible when working correctly

Citizens only notice the electrical grid or water pressure when it fails; flawless engineering silently fades into the background of daily life. Good governance and good SRE are identical: [[Observability is more than just logging]].[^1]

[^1]: Star, S. L. (1999). The Ethnography of Infrastructure. American Behavioral Scientist.
