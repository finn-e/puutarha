# Compassion requires active non-participation in systems of violence

Holding a peaceful internal philosophy means nothing if your daily purchasing habits directly fund slaughterhouses and exploitation. Aligning your actions with your morals demands that veganism becomes an active, daily practice.[^1]

[^1]: Singer, P. (1975). Animal Liberation.
