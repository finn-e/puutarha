# Preventative Maintenance for High-Torque Extruders

To prevent 'filament disk' formation and motor overload: 
1. Regularly inspect PTFE tube ends for deformation that increases friction.
2. Use compressed air to clear filament dust from the extruder intake.
3. Verify that retraction settings are not overly aggressive, which can shear the filament and leave debris in the gears.
