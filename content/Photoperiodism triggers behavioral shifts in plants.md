# Photoperiodism triggers behavioral shifts in plants

Plants possess biological clocks that track the exact ratio of daylight to darkness, triggering entirely new phases like flowering or dormancy based solely on solar angles. This reliance on light cycles mirrors how [[Sleep consolidation is critical for memory formation]].
