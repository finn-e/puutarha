# Multi-material printing requires precise purge calibration

When the Bambu AMS2 swaps filaments, it must extrude exact volumes of transitional plastic to prevent colors from bleeding into the model. Dialing this in perfectly is necessary, just as [[Automating toil prevents SRE burnout]].

[^1]: Bambu Lab. (2024). AMS Filament Flush Volume Documentation.
