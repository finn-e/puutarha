# Shared NHL venues optimize PWHL infrastructure

The Seattle Torrent sharing Climate Pledge Arena with the NHL's Seattle Kraken creates a massive statistical advantage in facilities management. Utilizing identical ice plants, broadcasting rigs, and transit infrastructure significantly lowers the operating friction for the expansion franchise. This operational efficiency is a textbook example of why [[System boundaries define what is analyzed]] when calculating franchise overhead.[^1]

[^1]: Climate Pledge Arena Operations and Tenant Logistics.
