# Sculpted keywells reduce ulnar deviation

Curving the physical PCB and switches into a three-dimensional bowl shape allows fingers to strike keys without bending the wrist outward. This engineering marvel acknowledges that [[Physics provides the ultimate hard constraints on engineering]].[^1]

[^1]: Kinesis Corporation. (1992). Ergonomic Design Principles of the Contoured Keyboard.
