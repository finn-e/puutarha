# The Zettelkasten method relies on decentralization

By avoiding rigid, top-down structures, individual notes link organically to form a bottom-up network graph. [[Open-source communities thrive on asynchronous collaboration]] using similar decentralized, trust-based networks.[^1]

[^1]: Luhmann, N. (1992). Communicating with Slip Boxes.
