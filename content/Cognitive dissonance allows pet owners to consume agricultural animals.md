# Cognitive dissonance allows pet owners to consume agricultural animals

The psychological mechanism that allows a human to lavish love on a dog while paying for the slaughter of a pig is profoundly fractured. Healing this dissonance requires adopting the stance that [[The abolitionist approach to animal rights demands veganism as a moral baseline]].[^1]

[^1]: Festinger, L. (1957). A Theory of Cognitive Dissonance.
