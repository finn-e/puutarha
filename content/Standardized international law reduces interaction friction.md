# Standardized international law reduces interaction friction

When different countries agree on shared banking protocols, airspace regulations, and trade tariffs, global cooperation can occur seamlessly without manual renegotiation. It is the diplomatic equivalent to how [[Asynchronous communication respects makers schedules]].[^1]

[^1]: Slaughter, A. M. (2004). A New World Order. Princeton University Press.
