# Dietary cholesterol is exclusively found in animal derived foods

Plants naturally produce zero cholesterol. Because the human liver synthesizes 100% of the cholesterol the body biologically requires, consuming external animal cholesterol only serves to toxify the bloodstream.[^1]

[^1]: American Heart Association. Dietary Cholesterol Guidelines.
