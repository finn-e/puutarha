# Microservices solve organizational rather than technical problems

Distributed architectures exist primarily to allow massive engineering teams to deploy independently without stepping on toes, despite the technical network overhead. Scaling organizations reveals that [[Comparative governance reveals system bottlenecks]].[^1]

[^1]: Conway, M. E. (1968). How do committees invent? Datamation. (Conway's Law)
