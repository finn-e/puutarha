# CI CD pipelines are the circulatory system of software

Automated testing and deployment networks are non-negotiable for high-velocity engineering; they catch regressions before they hit production. However, to keep builds fast, remember that [[Premature optimization is the root of all evil]].[^1]

[^1]: Humble, J., & Farley, D. (2010). Continuous Delivery. Addison-Wesley.
