# CoreXY kinematics enable high-speed deposition

By separating the heavy stepper motors from the moving toolhead, CoreXY printers like the Bambu P2S drastically reduce moving mass. This allows for extreme acceleration without ghosting or ringing on the final parts.[^1]

[^1]: CoreXY Kinematic Architecture Principles.
