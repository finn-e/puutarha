# Mathematical modeling bridges abstract math and physical reality

Differential equations allow us to perfectly predict the trajectory of a rocket or the flow of heat through a heatsink before it is built. This representation of reality is exactly how [[Embeddings represent semantic meaning mathematically]].[^1]

[^1]: Wigner, E. (1960). The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Mathematics in the Natural Sciences.
