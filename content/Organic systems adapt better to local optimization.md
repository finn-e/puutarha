# Organic systems adapt better to local optimization

Instead of requiring a centralized brain to command a root to grow left, the root simply follows the path of highest moisture and least physical resistance. This decentralized survival mechanism avoids the pitfalls where [[Local optimization often leads to global sub-optimization]].[^1]

[^1]: Mancuso, S., & Viola, A. (2015). Brilliant Green. Island Press.
