# 20gf actuation force drastically reduces repetitive strain injury risk

Standard keyboard switches require 45g-60g of downward force, rapidly exhausting tendon sheaths over millions of keystrokes. Using hyper-light 20gf springs allows typists to input commands with almost zero muscular effort.[^1]

[^1]: Ergonomic Analysis of Keystroke Actuation Forces.
