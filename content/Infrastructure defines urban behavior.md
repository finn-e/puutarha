# Infrastructure defines urban behavior

If a city builds multi-lane highways, citizens will drive; if a city builds protected, continuous cycling networks, citizens will bike. Concrete literally dictates daily routines, proving that [[Friction determines the likelihood of an action]].[^1]

[^1]: Montgomery, C. (2013). Happy City. Farrar, Straus and Giroux.
