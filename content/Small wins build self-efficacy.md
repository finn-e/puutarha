# Small wins build self-efficacy

Achieving tiny, low-stakes goals releases dopamine and proves to the brain that positive change is structurally possible. Over months, this compounds, exactly how [[Knowledge compounding acts like financial interest]].[^1]

[^1]: Weick, K. E. (1984). Small Wins. American Psychologist.
