# Thumb clusters offload heavy modifier usage from weak pinky fingers

Traditional keyboards force the weakest finger (the pinky) to repeatedly stretch for critical keys like Shift, Ctrl, and Enter. Ergonomic boards shift these high-use keys to the thumb, the strongest digit on the hand.[^1]

[^1]: Kinesis Contoured Keyboard Design Document.
