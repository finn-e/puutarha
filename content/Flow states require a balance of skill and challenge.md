# Flow states require a balance of skill and challenge

If a task is too easy, it induces boredom; if it is too hard, it induces anxiety. Hitting the optimal zone triggers peak engagement, similar to how [[Meaningful choices in games require permanent consequences]].[^1]

[^1]: Csikszentmihalyi, M. (1990). Flow.
