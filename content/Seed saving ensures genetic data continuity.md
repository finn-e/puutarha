# Seed saving ensures genetic data continuity

Propagating your own heirloom seeds year over year insulates your garden from proprietary, single-generation agricultural monopolies. This organic preservation of history is the exact biological equivalent to how [[Plain text guarantees data longevity]].[^1]

[^1]: Ashworth, S. (2002). Seed to Seed. Seed Savers Exchange.
