# Plant based diets reverse arterial plaque buildup

Clinical interventions have demonstrated that adopting a strict, low-fat, whole-food vegan diet can not only halt the progression of coronary artery disease but actively re-open narrowed arteries. This incredible medical reality proves that [[Veganism proves that human health does not require animal exploitation]].[^1]

[^1]: Ornish, D., et al. (1990). Can lifestyle changes reverse coronary heart disease? The Lancet.
