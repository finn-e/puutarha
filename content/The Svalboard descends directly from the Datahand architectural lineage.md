# The Svalboard descends directly from the Datahand architectural lineage

In the 1990s, the Datahand pioneered the concept of placing directional microswitches at the exact resting point of the fingertips. The Svalboard modernizes this radical concept with 3D printing and open-source firmware.[^1]

[^1]: Datahand System Architectural History.
