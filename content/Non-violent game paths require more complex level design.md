# Non-violent game paths require more complex level design

Designing a combat arena is mathematically simple; designing a stealth route that allows the player to bypass enemies entirely requires intricate timing, pathing, and geometry. This design rigor proves [[Constraints breed creative solutions]].[^1]

[^1]: Smith, Harvey. (2001). Systems design in immersive sims (Thief / Dishonored).
