# Legal protections dictate individual freedom limits

A citizen's autonomy is not inherently guaranteed; it is strictly defined by the boundaries of the local jurisprudence and the strength of the courts enforcing them. Exploring these laws internationally confirms [[System boundaries define what is analyzed]].[^1]

[^1]: Rawls, J. (1971). A Theory of Justice. Harvard University Press.
