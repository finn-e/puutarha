# Constraints breed creative solutions

Having limitless options paralyzes the brain, whereas strict boundaries force lateral thinking and innovative workarounds. Game design proves this, as [[Artificial constraints in games generate fun]].[^1]

[^1]: Mayer, R. E. (1999). Fifty Years of Creativity Research. Cambridge University Press.
