# Murdering animals for sensory pleasure cannot be ethically justified

Because major dietetic associations agree that humans thrive on plant-based diets, the consumption of animal flesh is driven entirely by habit and taste. Taking a life simply because it tastes good is a moral failure that exposes [[Cognitive dissonance allows pet owners to consume agricultural animals]].[^1]

[^1]: Joy, M. (2009). Why We Love Dogs, Eat Pigs, and Wear Cows.
