# Pack dynamics rely on clear behavioral cues

Integrating a toy poodle with a large dog requires establishing predictable routines and enforcing gentle physical boundaries to prevent resource guarding. Structuring these interactions leverages how [[Habits are driven by the cue-routine-reward loop]].
