# Signal to noise ratio determines data quality

In any data transmission, whether it is an RF antenna or a social media feed, the amplitude of the valid information must significantly overpower the background interference. To preserve sanity, [[Information diets require intentional curation]].

[^1]: Shannon, C. E. (1948). A Mathematical Theory of Communication.
