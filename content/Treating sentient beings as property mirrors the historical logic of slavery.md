# Treating sentient beings as property mirrors the historical logic of slavery

Legal frameworks that classify feeling, suffering beings as commodities or resources inherently strip them of their fundamental rights to life and autonomy. This systemic commodification relies on the same psychological mechanisms that historically justified human slavery.[^1]

[^1]: Francione, G. L. (1995). Animals, Property, and the Law.
