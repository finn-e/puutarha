# Plant based diets systemically lower baseline inflammation markers

Due to the massive influx of antioxidants and the elimination of pro-inflammatory arachidonic acid found in chicken and eggs, vegan diets drastically lower CRP levels, reducing joint pain and chronic disease.[^1]

[^1]: Sutliffe, J. T., et al. (2015). C-reactive protein response to a vegan lifestyle intervention.
