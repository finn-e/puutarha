# Olympic tournaments introduce roster volatility in the PWHL

During an Olympic year like 2026, PWHL teams face severe statistical disruptions as top players leave for international duty and return with heightened injury risks. When Hilary Knight sustained a lower-body injury at the 2026 Milano Cortina games, she was placed on long-term injured reserve for the Torrent.
