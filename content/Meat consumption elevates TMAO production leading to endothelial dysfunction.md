# Meat consumption elevates TMAO production leading to endothelial dysfunction

When gut bacteria metabolize the L-carnitine found in red meat, the liver converts it into TMAO, a compound highly toxic to the endothelial cells lining the arteries. Plant-based eaters do not produce these toxic spikes.[^1]

[^1]: Koeth, R. A., et al. (2013). Intestinal microbiota metabolism of L-carnitine.
