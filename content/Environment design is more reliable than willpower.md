# Environment design is more reliable than willpower

Willpower is a depleting resource; physically shaping your surroundings to make good habits easy and bad habits hard guarantees long-term success. Similarly, [[Animal behavior is driven by environmental design]].[^1]

[^1]: Thaler, R. H., & Sunstein, C. R. (2008). Nudge. Yale University Press.
