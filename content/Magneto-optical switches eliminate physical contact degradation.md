# Magneto-optical switches eliminate physical contact degradation

Because they rely on light beams and magnets rather than scraping copper leaves together, magneto-optical switches never suffer from debounce issues or contact corrosion. They provide incredibly smooth 20gf actuation infinitely.[^1]
