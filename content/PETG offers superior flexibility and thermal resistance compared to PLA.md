# PETG offers superior flexibility and thermal resistance compared to PLA

While PLA is easy to print, it deforms under low heat and is highly brittle. PETG requires higher temperatures but yields parts that can withstand mechanical stress and environmental heat, making it vastly superior for functional keyboard cases.[^1]

[^1]: Material Science of FDM Polymers.
