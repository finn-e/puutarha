# AI hallucinations are a feature of generative creativity

Generating plausible but logically false text relies on the exact same neural pathways as generating creative fiction. Rather than a bug, it proves that [[Constraints breed creative solutions]].

[^1]: Ji, Z., et al. (2023). Survey of Hallucination in Natural Language Generation. ACM Computing Surveys.
