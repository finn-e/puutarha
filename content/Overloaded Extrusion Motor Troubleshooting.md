# Overloaded Extrusion Motor Troubleshooting

The 'Overloaded Extrusion Motor' error is a safety shutoff triggered when the stepper motor's torque limit is exceeded. Troubleshooting should follow a 'path of least resistance' logic: start with the filament spool, move to the hotend, and finally internalize to the extruder gear assembly. A key indicator of mechanical vs. electrical failure is the presence of a 'clicking' sound, which confirms the motor is receiving power but meeting physical resistance.
