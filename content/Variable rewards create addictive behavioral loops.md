# Variable rewards create addictive behavioral loops

Unpredictable payouts trigger massive dopamine spikes, cementing behaviors far stronger than reliable, predictable rewards. Video game design relies entirely on this, where [[RNG based trophies violate the implicit contract of effort based rewards]].[^1]

[^1]: Skinner, B. F. (1953). Science and Human Behavior.
