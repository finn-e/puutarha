# Therapeutic diets are precise biological interventions

Prescription foods, like a urinary diet for a cat like Ceartas, utilize strict mineral restrictions and pH balancers to actively dissolve crystals and prevent blockages. Controlling this biological chemistry mimics how [[Pepper cultivation requires strict thermal regulation]].
