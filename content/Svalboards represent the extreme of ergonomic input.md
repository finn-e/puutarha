# Svalboards represent the extreme of ergonomic input

Utilizing light-action magneto-optical switches and radical 3D spatial alignment, the Svalboard is the ultimate descendant of the Datahand, eliminating nearly all finger travel distance and strain.[^1]

[^1]: Svalboard Documentation (svalboard.com).
