# Enrichment activities prevent cognitive decline in pets

Forcing animals to solve puzzles to acquire their food engages their working memory and prevents the destructive behaviors born from chronic boredom. Stimulating their brains ensures [[Flow states require a balance of skill and challenge]].

[^1]: Meehan, M. A., & Mench, J. A. (2007). The challenge of challenge: can problem solving opportunities enhance animal welfare? Applied Animal Behaviour Science.
