# Bed adhesion is a function of thermodynamics and surface area

If the temperature differential between the heated bed and the cooling plastic is too high, the polymer shrinks, curling off the build plate. Controlling this warp proves that [[Thermodynamics governs system efficiency]].[^1]

[^1]: Bambu Lab. (2024). Thermal Management in Enclosed Printers.
