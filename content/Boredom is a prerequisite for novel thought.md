# Boredom is a prerequisite for novel thought

Constant digital stimulation prevents the mind from wandering, robbing the subconscious of the time it needs to process complex associations. To reclaim this capability, [[Dopamine detoxes reset reward baselines]].[^1]

[^1]: Manoush, Z. (2017). Bored and Brilliant. St. Martin's Press.
