# Mind mapping bypasses linear thinking

Visually charting concepts on a 2D plane mimics the brain's associative nature, revealing relationships hidden by standard linear outlines. It visually demonstrates how [[Complex systems exhibit emergent properties]].

[^1]: Buzan, T. (1993). The Mind Map Book. BBC Books.
