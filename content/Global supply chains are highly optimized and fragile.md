# Global supply chains are highly optimized and fragile

Just-in-time international shipping completely eliminated warehouse overhead but ensured that a single boat stuck in a canal could crash global commerce for months. This vulnerability perfectly illustrates that [[Local optimization often leads to global sub-optimization]].[^1]

[^1]: Christopher, M. (2016). Logistics & Supply Chain Management. Pearson.
