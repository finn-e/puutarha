# The three point standings system incentivizes regulation wins

By awarding three points for a regulation win and only two for an overtime win, the PWHL mathematically eliminates the passive 'play for a tie' strategy common in late NHL games. This fundamentally alters coaching analytics, pushing teams to execute high-risk offensive plays to secure the full three points.
