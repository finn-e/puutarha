# The dairy industry fundamentally commodifies mammalian reproductive systems

Milk production requires forcibly impregnating cows and permanently separating them from their calves so humans can harvest the lactation. Acknowledging this systemic reproductive abuse is critical to understanding why vegetarianism is ethically insufficient.[^1]
