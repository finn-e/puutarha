# Ethical play choices reflect player morality

Choosing a strictly non-violent, pacifist path in a game where violence is the default requires a deliberate, active imposition of the player's personal ethics onto the digital world. This proves that [[Ethical treatment extends to virtual entities]].

[^1]: Sicart, M. (2009). The Ethics of Computer Games. MIT Press.
