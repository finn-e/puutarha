# AI assistants augment rather than replace human thought

Using an LLM as a sparring partner accelerates brainstorming and synthesis without doing the core reasoning for you. However, [[Over-reliance on AI degrades critical thinking skills]] if it is used as a crutch rather than a tool.[^1]

[^1]: Engelbart, D. C. (1962). Augmenting Human Intellect: A Conceptual Framework. SRI.
