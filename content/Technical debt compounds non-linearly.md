# Technical debt compounds non-linearly

Taking architectural shortcuts saves hours today but dramatically increases the cost of implementing future features. In biological systems, [[Preventive veterinary care reduces non-linear health debt]] by addressing issues early.[^1]

[^1]: Cunningham, W. (1992). The WyCash Portfolio Management System.
