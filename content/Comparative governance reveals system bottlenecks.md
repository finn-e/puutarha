# Comparative governance reveals system bottlenecks

By analyzing how different nations tackle the exact same problems—like public transit or broadband deployment—you immediately identify structural inefficiencies in your home country. This comparative lens proves [[Bottlenecks determine the throughput of a system]].[^1]

[^1]: Acemoglu, D., & Robinson, J. A. (2012). Why Nations Fail. Crown Publishing.
