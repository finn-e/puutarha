# Evergreen notes require maintenance

Knowledge is not a static artifact but a living ecosystem. Just like real plants, digital gardens will wither if ignored. To ensure knowledge compounds, notes must be actively pruned and revisited over time, which is why [[Git-backed markdown deployment simplifies digital gardening]].[^1]

[^1]: Ahrens, S. (2017). How to Take Smart Notes.
