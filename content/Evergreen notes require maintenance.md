# Evergreen notes require maintenance

Knowledge is not a static artifact but a living ecosystem. Just like real plants, [[Digital gardens mimic organic biological growth]] and will wither if ignored. To ensure [[Knowledge compounding acts like financial interest]], notes must be actively pruned and revisited over time.[^1]

[^1]: Ahrens, S. (2017). How to Take Smart Notes. Createspace Independent Publishing.
