# Tenting angles neutralize forearm pronation

Typing on a flat keyboard forces the radius and ulna bones in the forearm to cross, creating chronic tension. Tenting the keyboard halves at steep angles allows the hands to rest vertically, in a natural handshake position.[^1]

[^1]: Biomechanics of Forearm Pronation in Typists.
