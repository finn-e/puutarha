# Complex systems exhibit emergent properties

The behavior of a whole system cannot be understood merely by dissecting its individual parts; new dynamics arise purely from their interaction. In nature, [[Soil microbiomes act as complex network graphs]] producing emergent health.[^1]

[^1]: Meadows, D. H. (2008). Thinking in Systems: A Primer. Chelsea Green.
