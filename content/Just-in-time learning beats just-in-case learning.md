# Just-in-time learning beats just-in-case learning

Consuming information at the exact moment you need to apply it ensures high relevance, immediate context, and high retention. In SRE, this is critical because [[Observability is more than just logging]].[^1]

[^1]: Toyota Production System (Origins of JIT).
