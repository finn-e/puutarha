# High scoring games require converging statistical anomalies

The PWHL is a notoriously heavy, defensive league, meaning high-scoring matches require simultaneous breakdowns in structural play and massive spikes in shooting percentages. When the Seattle Torrent defeated Toronto 6-4 in January 2026, it set a franchise record fueled heavily by early power-play capitalization. Tracking these statistical outliers helps models predict when [[Systems decay without active maintenance]] on the blue line.[^1]
