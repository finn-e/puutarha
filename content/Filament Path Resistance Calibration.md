# Filament Path Resistance Calibration

Total resistance in the extrusion system is the sum of spool friction + PTFE drag + extruder gear friction + nozzle backpressure. When troubleshooting an overload, isolate these variables by removing the PTFE tube and manually feeding filament to feel for 'snag' points.
