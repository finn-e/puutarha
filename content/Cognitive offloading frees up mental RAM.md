# Cognitive offloading frees up mental RAM

Writing tasks down in an external, trusted system saves the brain from wasting energy trying to remember them. For example, [[Canine pack dynamics require structured automatic feeding]] by offloading pet care memory.[^1]

[^1]: Risko, E. F. (2016). Cognitive Offloading.
