# Bodily autonomy is a fundamental right that extends to non-human animals

Just as human rights frameworks are built on the inviolability of the individual's body, sentient animals possess a profound interest in continuing their own lives free from pain and captivity. Recognizing this right is an extension of universal justice.[^1]

[^1]: Regan, T. (1983). The Case for Animal Rights.
