# Error budgets align dev and ops incentives

By mathematically defining an acceptable level of failure, developers can ship fast until the budget is spent, at which point focus must shift to reliability. This framework prevents how the [[Tragedy of the commons arises from misaligned incentives]].[^1]

[^1]: Beyer, B., et al. (2016). Site Reliability Engineering. O'Reilly Media.
