# Perfectionism is a form of procrastination

Waiting for flawless conditions or endlessly refining details is often a fear-based delay tactic preventing shipping. To overcome this, remember that [[The first draft is just telling yourself the story]].[^1]

[^1]: Burka, J. B., & Yuen, L. M. (2008). Procrastination: Why You Do It, What to Do About It.
