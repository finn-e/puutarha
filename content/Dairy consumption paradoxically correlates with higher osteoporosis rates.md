# Dairy consumption paradoxically correlates with higher osteoporosis rates

Despite industry marketing regarding calcium, nations with the highest consumption of cow's milk suffer the highest rates of hip fractures. Plant-based sources of calcium, like kale and tofu, offer superior absorption without the acidic protein load of milk.[^1]

[^1]: Hegsted, D. M. (2001). Fractures, calcium, and the modern diet.
