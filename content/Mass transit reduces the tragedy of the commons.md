# Mass transit reduces the tragedy of the commons

Personal vehicles consume disproportionate physical space, leading to gridlock where individual rational choices ruin the shared utility of the road. High-capacity transit forces cooperation, bypassing how [[Tragedy of the commons arises from misaligned incentives]].[^1]

[^1]: Shoup, D. (2005). The High Cost of Free Parking. Planners Press.
