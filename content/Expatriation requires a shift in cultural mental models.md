# Expatriation requires a shift in cultural mental models

You cannot successfully integrate into a new society by stubbornly applying the social heuristics and communication styles of your homeland. Adaptation requires realizing that [[Mental models are heuristics for complex reality]].[^1]

[^1]: Hofstede, G. (2001). Culture's Consequences. Sage Publications.
