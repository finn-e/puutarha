# Plain text guarantees data longevity

Proprietary formats like DOCX or PDF rely on complex, corporate-owned rendering engines that will eventually become obsolete. Storing your mind in Markdown ensures that [[Evergreen notes require maintenance]] by you, not by a corporation.[^1]

[^1]: Hunt, A., & Thomas, D. (1999). The Pragmatic Programmer. Addison-Wesley.
