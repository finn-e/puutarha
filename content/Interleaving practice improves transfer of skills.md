# Interleaving practice improves transfer of skills

Mixing different subjects or problem types during a single study session dramatically improves the brain's ability to choose the correct strategy later. [[Custom keyboards blend electrical engineering with craft]], demanding this exact cross-disciplinary transfer.[^1]

[^1]: Rohrer, D. (2012). Interleaving helps students distinguish among similar concepts. Educational Psychology Review.
