# Interleaving practice improves transfer of skills

Mixing different subjects or problem types during a single study session dramatically improves the brain's ability to choose the correct strategy later. Building custom hardware demands this exact cross-disciplinary transfer.[^1]

[^1]: Rohrer, D. (2012). Interleaving helps students.
