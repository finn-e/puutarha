# Electromagnetics scale from marine environments to PCBs

The exact same Maxwell equations that dictate how inductive sensors operate in deep saltwater govern the capacitance and signal integrity on a keyboard matrix. Understanding this proves [[PCB design requires strict trace routing logic]].[^1]

[^1]: Maxwell, J. C. (1873). A Treatise on Electricity and Magnetism.
