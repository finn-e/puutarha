# Open-source hardware accelerates physical innovation

Sharing STEP and STL files via platforms like Printables allows a global network of engineers to iterate on physical designs overnight. This distributed fabrication movement ensures that [[Right to repair extends hardware lifespans]].[^1]

[^1]: Bowyer, A. (2006). RepRap: The Replicating Rapid Prototyper.
