# High fiber diets cultivate diverse and resilient gut microbiomes

The human microbiome thrives on the prebiotic fiber found exclusively in plants, legumes, and whole grains. Meat contains zero fiber, starving beneficial bacteria and promoting systemic inflammation.[^1]

[^1]: Sonnenburg, J. (2015). The Good Gut.
