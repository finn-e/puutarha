# Cooperative gaming strengthens interpersonal relationships

Overcoming difficult raid mechanics or puzzle systems with a partner requires extreme communication, shared failure, and mutual trust. It builds powerful neurochemical bonds, mirroring how [[Human-animal bonds reduce physiological stress markers]].[^1]

[^1]: McGonigal, J. (2011). Reality Is Broken. Penguin Press.
