# Slow travel reconnects spatial awareness

Taking a multi-day Amtrak journey forces the brain to process the true physical scale of geography, unlike the teleportation-like abstraction of air travel. Disconnecting from speed lowers cortisol, much like how [[Digital clutter induces background anxiety]].

[^1]: Macfarlane, R. (2012). The Old Ways: A Journey on Foot. Hamish Hamilton.
