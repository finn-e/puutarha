# Elite goaltending acts as the ultimate defensive redundancy

In the PWHL, elite goaltenders completely warp expected goals (xG) statistics, single-handedly stealing games where their team is massively outshot. When the Seattle Torrent face goalies like Boston's Aerin Frankel, their offensive shooting percentages plummet well below the mean. Securing a statistically dominant goalie proves that [[Redundancy prevents single points of failure]] when the defensive structure breaks down.[^1]

[^1]: PWHL Advanced Goaltending Metrics and Expected Goals (xG).
