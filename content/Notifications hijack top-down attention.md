# Notifications hijack top-down attention

Non-essential alerts force the brain to abandon deep, deliberate focus to process a trivial, bottom-up interruption. Disabling them is mandatory because [[Context switching drains executive function]].[^1]

[^1]: Newport, C. (2019). Digital Minimalism. Portfolio.
