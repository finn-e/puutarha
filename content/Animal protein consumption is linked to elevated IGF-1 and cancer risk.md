# Animal protein consumption is linked to elevated IGF-1 and cancer risk

The specific amino acid profiles found in animal protein trigger the liver to produce excess Insulin-like Growth Factor 1 (IGF-1), a hormone strongly correlated with the promotion of tumor growth in adults.[^1]

[^1]: Longo, V. D., et al. (2014). Low protein intake is associated with a major reduction in IGF-1.
