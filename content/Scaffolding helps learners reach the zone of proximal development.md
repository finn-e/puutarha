# Scaffolding helps learners reach the zone of proximal development

Providing temporary frameworks allows a learner to achieve tasks they could not do independently, progressively removing the support as competence grows. [[Games are sophisticated interactive state machines]] that seamlessly scaffold tutorials.[^1]

[^1]: Vygotsky, L. S. (1978). Mind in Society. Harvard University Press.
