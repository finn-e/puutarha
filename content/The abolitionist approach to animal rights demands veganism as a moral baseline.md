# The abolitionist approach to animal rights demands veganism as a moral baseline

If non-human animals possess any intrinsic moral value whatsoever, we cannot ethically justify exploiting them for non-essential human pleasure or convenience. Therefore, embracing veganism is not merely a dietary choice, but the absolute minimum baseline of justice.[^1]

[^1]: Francione, G. L. Animal Rights: The Abolitionist Approach.
