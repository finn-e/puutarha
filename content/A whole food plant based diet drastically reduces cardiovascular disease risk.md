# A whole food plant based diet drastically reduces cardiovascular disease risk

Eliminating dietary cholesterol and severely restricting saturated fats found in meat and dairy directly correlates with plummeted rates of heart attacks. The epidemiological evidence proves that human longevity thrives on plants.[^1]

[^1]: Esselstyn, C. B. (2007). Prevent and Reverse Heart Disease.
