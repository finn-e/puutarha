# Pruning stimulates productive biological growth

Severing dead, dying, or overcrowded branches forces the plant to redirect its finite hydraulic pressure and nutrients exclusively into new fruiting bodies. Similarly, [[Information diets require intentional curation]] to protect cognitive energy.
