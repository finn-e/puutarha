# Rapid prototyping accelerates the feedback loop

Holding a physical object in your hands reveals volumetric and ergonomic flaws that are entirely invisible on a CAD screen. This immediate validation loop demonstrates how [[Feedback loops drive system behavior]].[^1]

[^1]: Ries, E. (2011). The Lean Startup. Crown Business.
