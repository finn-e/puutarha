# Burnout is inevitable when intrinsic motivation is replaced by extrinsic trophies

If the only reason a player boots up a console is to watch a digital counter increase, the joy of the underlying mechanical systems rapidly evaporates. Recognizing this psychological cliff is crucial to knowing when to retire.[^1]

[^1]: Deci, E. L. (1985). Intrinsic Motivation and Self-Determination.
