# Inspiration is a byproduct of doing the work

Waiting to feel motivated before beginning a project guarantees failure. Taking physical action generates momentum, which triggers inspiration, validating that [[The streak effect builds momentum]].[^1]

[^1]: Close, C. (2003). Interview on inspiration vs. work ethic.
