# Veganism recognizes sentience as the sole prerequisite for moral consideration

An organism does not need to possess human-level intelligence to deserve freedom from torture; it simply needs the neurological capacity to experience pain and subjective reality. Sentience is the only line that matters.[^1]

[^1]: The Cambridge Declaration on Consciousness (2012).
