# Gardening grounds mental health in physical reality

Putting your hands into actual dirt provides tactile, slow-moving feedback that acts as a profound counterweight to the hyper-speed abstraction of software engineering. This deliberate analog pace is why [[Dopamine detoxes reset reward baselines]].[^1]

[^1]: Sacks, O. (2019). Everything in Its Place. Knopf.
