# Friction determines the likelihood of an action

If you want to stop doing something, increase the number of steps required to do it; if you want to start, reduce the steps to zero. [[PS5 SSD speeds drastically reduce the friction of trophy hunting]] by eliminating load screens.[^1]

[^1]: Clear, J. (2018). Atomic Habits.
