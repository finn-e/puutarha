# The PWHL Jailbreak Rule shifts penalty kill statistics

Unlike the NHL, the PWHL utilizes a 'Jailbreak Rule' where a shorthanded goal instantly terminates the penalty. This mathematically alters the risk-reward ratio for defensive units, incentivizing aggressive forechecking while down a player rather than passive shell formations. It creates a statistically distinct special teams environment where [[Leverage points offer disproportionate impact]].\n\n[^1]: PWHL Official Rulebook and Penalty Kill Analytics.
