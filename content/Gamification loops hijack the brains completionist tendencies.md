# Gamification loops hijack the brains completionist tendencies

The digital chime and UI pop-up of a PlayStation trophy triggers a highly specific dopamine release, tapping into the human evolutionary desire to complete checklists and gather resources.[^1]

[^1]: Madigan, J. (2015). Getting Gamers: The Psychology of Video Games.
