# Premature optimization is the root of all evil

Spending resources making a function faster before proving it is actually the system's bottleneck is a massive waste of engineering effort. This is why [[Trunk-based development forces continuous integration]] to prioritize working features first.

[^1]: Knuth, D. (1974). Computer Programming as an Art. Communications of the ACM.
