# RNG based trophies violate the implicit contract of effort based rewards

When a Platinum requires a 0.5% random drop rate from an enemy rather than demonstrating mechanical skill, the game stops testing competence and begins testing pure endurance. This inevitably leads to extreme frustration.[^1]

[^1]: Game Design Principles for Rewarding Player Investment.
