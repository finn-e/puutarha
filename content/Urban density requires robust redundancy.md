# Urban density requires robust redundancy

If a city relies on a single subway line or a single bridge to move millions, any minor disruption cascades into city-wide gridlock. Planners must build overlapping grids because [[Redundancy prevents single points of failure]].

[^1]: Jacobs, J. (1961). The Death and Life of Great American Cities. Random House.
