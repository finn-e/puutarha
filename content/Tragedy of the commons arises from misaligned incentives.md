# Tragedy of the commons arises from misaligned incentives

When individuals act purely rationally in their own self-interest, they will inevitably deplete a shared public resource, leading to collective ruin. Preventing this requires overarching management; [[Social safety nets act as systemic redundancy]].[^1]

[^1]: Hardin, G. (1968). The Tragedy of the Commons. Science.
